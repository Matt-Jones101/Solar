Earth texture tiers are resized from Natural Earth II
`NE2_50M_LC_SR_W`, the no-label shaded-relief and water raster.

Source package:
`https://naciscdn.org/naturalearth/50m/raster/NE2_50M_LC_SR_W.zip`

These generated app assets intentionally keep natural geography only:
terrain, water, ice, lakes, coastlines, and relief. They do not include
cities, roads, borders, or labels.
